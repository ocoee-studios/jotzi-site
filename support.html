Common questions

How does pricing work?

Android: Jotzi is a one-time paid download on Google Play. All features are included — there are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Apple pricing. The upcoming iPhone and Apple Watch update will cost $0.99 as a one-time U.S. App Store download. All features are included, with no trial, subscription, or in-app purchases.

Apple version 1.2.1. The free app includes core features. Jotzi Pro is available as a subscription through the App Store. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or visit App Store Subscriptions.

How do I restore Jotzi Pro in Apple version 1.2.1?

Open Jotzi → More → Jotzi Pro → tap "Restore purchases." This checks your Apple ID for an active Jotzi Pro subscription. On Android, all features are already included.

Where is my data stored?

All your reminders, notes, lists, and settings are stored locally on your device. Jotzi does not have cloud sync. If you delete the app, your data will be removed. Use the Export feature (Jotzi Pro) to back up your data.

How do I export my data?

Open Jotzi → More → Settings → "Export / back up my Jotzi data." In Apple version 1.2.1, this is a Jotzi Pro feature. On Android, export is included with the app.
