TITLE: Jotzi — Reminder Companion

SUBTITLE: Reminders, notes, lists and a friend.

DESCRIPTION:

Jotzi keeps the stuff you are trying not to forget out of your head.

Create reminders with one tap. Choose Later today, Tonight or Tomorrow. Turn on Keep reminding me when you actually want Jotzi to stay on you about something. Mark things done from Home, remind yourself again, or move something to tomorrow.

Everything is included with your download — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no feature locks.

REMINDERS
• Create a reminder in seconds
• Schedule for later today, tonight or tomorrow
• Keep reminding me for the things that matter
• Mark done, remind again or move to tomorrow
• Reminder history so nothing is lost

NOTES & LISTS
• Quick notes for the things that don't need a due date
• Lists for groceries, packing, errands — anything
• Templates for things you do more than once

JOTZI COMPANION
• Choose your Jotzi character from the Spirit Shelf
• Daily Check-In to set how your day feels
• 18 companions — 7 everyday and 11 seasonal

CALENDAR
• See your reminder days with the matching Jotzi character
• Tap a day to see what Jotzi is holding for you

APPLE WATCH (iOS)
• Reminder actions right on your wrist
• Done, Remind Again, Tomorrow and Stop
• Matching Jotzi character on Watch

APPLE HEALTH (iOS)
• Optional read-only access to sleep, steps and mindful minutes
• Wellness context — never medical advice

PRIVACY
• All data stays on your device
• No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking
• Face ID / Touch ID App Lock
• Data export anytime
